Roadmap

Two pillars, built together

Flywheel advances its tooling and its library together, because each one drives the other.

Pillar 1AI4Math tooling for Lean

Open agents, provers, and harnesses for autoformalization and proof search, with the benchmarks and data pipelines to evaluate and improve them.

Pillar 2A unified library of computer science

One coherent library covering CS foundations, verified algorithms and data structures, semantics, and more, grown with the Pillar 1 tooling.

Directions
