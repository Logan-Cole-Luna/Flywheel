Governance

A steering committee sets direction and priorities; a maintainer team owns the tools and their releases.

Steering committee

The steering committee sets the direction and priorities of the project and will include a representative of each committed partner.

Vijay Ganesh Professor of Computer Science, Georgia Tech; Director, AI4Math Center Venkat Guruswami Professor of EECS and Mathematics, UC Berkeley; Director, Simons Institute for the Theory of Computing

Maintainers

Maintainers own the tools’ technical direction and releases, and are invited on the basis of sustained contributions.

Logan Luna Maintainer; Georgia Tech

Reference definitions

Each reference is proposed with its source in the literature, reviewed by domain experts and the relevant Mathlib or CSLib maintainers, and versioned once accepted.

Contributions

All code is reviewed on GitHub. AI agents may propose code, bridges, and references; a human maintainer approves every merge.

Vijay Ganesh, Georgia Tech. A Flywheel channel on the Lean Zulip will be announced.
